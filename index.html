GasFlo

Automated LPG dispensing system

A customer buys LPG and gets a token. The machine validates the token, checks the cylinder is in place, weighs it, dispenses with live progress, stops at the purchased quantity and prints a receipt.

  • Designed and tested the prototype and the custom PCB.
  • Wrote the ESP32-S3 firmware for weighing, cylinder detection, dispensing control and live updates.
  • Integrated the pre-designed ESP32-P4 touchscreen, backend token validation and BLE receipt printing.
  • Validated the full workflow and helped deploy four operational units.

CNG Remote Data Capturing

Industrial gas-metering telemetry

Edge gateways that read industrial gas meters at CNG facilities and make the data available remotely.

  • Commissioned 4 Raspberry Pi gateways, now active, with Python polling of PTZ meters over Modbus RTU on RS485.
  • Captured pressure, temperature, measured volume (Vm), corrected volume (Vb) and interval readings.
  • Set up Linux services, networking and remote access; fixed power, meter-comms and connectivity faults on site.
  • Installed, commissioned, validated and handed over with documentation.

How I work

Requirements in, a deployed system out

The same six stages carry every project, and the last one is where most prototypes stall.

  1. Requirements

    What the machine must do, measure and survive on site.

  2. Architecture

    MCU, sensors, buses and the path to the cloud.

  3. Design

    Schematic, PCB layout and firmware structure.

  4. Prototype

    Board bring-up, drivers and first integration.

  5. Validation

    Test the full workflow, fix faults, document results.

  6. Deployment

    Install, commission, hand over and support.
